Dadgad — Music March Madness

Dadgad is an interactive music discovery application that brings the excitement of March Madness to music listening. Inspired by tournament-style brackets, the application lets users compare tracks head-to-head, advance their favorites through successive rounds, and ultimately crown a champion.

Built with React, Next.js, Django, and PostgreSQL, Dadgad combines an interactive frontend with a backend designed to manage bracket progression, persist tournament state, and handle the logic behind advancing tracks across rounds. Integration with the Apple Music API connects the experience to a broader music catalog, while AI-generated commentary adds another layer of personality to the competition.

The project explores the intersection of music discovery, interactive experiences, and full-stack application architecture, with an emphasis on turning subjective listening preferences into a structured, competitive, and engaging experience.
